The totals chain

Everything an order says about money is either a link in this chain or the breakdown of one link.

line items ──minus discounts──▶ subtotal ──plus shipping, tax, tip──▶ total │ │ │ │ price × quantity │ never contains tax, │ what the card │ per line │ shipping or tips │ was charged

The formulas, in plain words

line value             = price × quantity − total_discount

total_line_items_price = sum of (price × quantity) over all lines

subtotal_price         = sum of line value over all lines
                       = total_line_items_price − sum of total_discount

shipping               = sum of discounted_price over all shipping lines

total_discounts        = sum of total_discount over all lines
                         + sum of shipping discounts

total_tax              = sum of tax_lines.price over all lines
                         + sum of tax_lines.price over all shipping lines

total_price            = subtotal_price + shipping + total_tax + total_tip_received
                         (leave total_tax out when taxes_included is true)

current_total_price    = total_price − refunds + order edits
⚠ Two traps
1. total_discounts includes shipping discounts, but shipping already enters through discounted_price. Subtracting total_discounts from anything that contains shipping counts the discount twice.
2. subtotal_price never contains tax, shipping or tips. That is why it is the natural cashback base.
